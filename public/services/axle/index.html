THE WORK

Focused on the details that matter.

Unusual noises or a clunk when shifting can point to axle or differential trouble. The team can inspect the vehicle, diagnose the cause and recommend the next repair.

Services and areas of work

  • Axle inspections and repairs
  • Differential diagnosis
  • Passenger and light-duty trucks
  • 4WD and off-road vehicles
GOOD TO KNOW

Signs to watch for

Call the shop if you are unsure which service fits your vehicle. The team can discuss the problem and advise on the next step.

  1. High-pitched noise under acceleration
  2. Dragging sound while coasting
  3. Clunk between drive and reverse
  4. Loud noise while turning
